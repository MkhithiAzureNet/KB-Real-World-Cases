# Naming Conventions

- Use descriptive Markdown filenames with hyphens between words.
- Prefer names that identify the product or topic and the specific issue or task.
- Keep each entry in the most relevant category and subcategory.
- Use `README.md` for folder indexes and scope descriptions.
- Avoid dates in filenames unless a date is essential to distinguish versions or events; record event dates in the document instead.
- Do not include names, email addresses, hostnames, tenant identifiers, or other sensitive values in filenames.

Examples:

- `entra-id-conditional-access-lockout.md`
- `linux-disk-space-investigation.md`
- `printer-driver-deployment.md`
